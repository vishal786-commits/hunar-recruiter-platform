About Us

The best way to predict the future is to invent it. And the best way to invent the future is to

get the best minds to work on an idea whose time has come.

Evam Labs (www.evamlabs.ai) is a Singapore headquartered holding company with

offices in Bangalore and San Jose, building the next generation of high impact AI

powered Enterprise Solutions. From Asia, for the World.

The first two companies being built by Evam Labs are:

● Propheus is on a mission to build the most comprehensive knowledge

representation of every place on the Planet. The company’s solutions are powering

various use cases across industries such as Retail, CPG, Real Estate and Government.

● Poiro is building the next generation of AI Agents for Marketers to bring Brands

closer to their customers.

Our thesis is simple: How Enterprises leverage data to get insights and make decisions

will get massively disrupted with AI creating a $1T+ opportunity. Evam Labs is at the

forefront of this disruption.

Evam Labs was founded by ex-founders, academics and investors with over a decade of

experience in building data & AI products and scaling companies from 0 to IPO. The

founders are IIT/IIM/CMU alumni and have cumulatively raised over $500M+, invested in 30+

startups and hold 20+ patents. The rest of the Evam team comprises alumni of IITs, IIMs,

CMU, IISc, NUS with rich experience across multiple industries.

Role Overview - AI Engineer

The AI Engineer is a hands-on role, positioned at the intersection of AI innovation,

engineering excellence, and customer impact. You won't just build systems; you'll

transform complex business challenges into simple, elegant, and scalable AI

solutions. You will be instrumental in transforming nascent ideas into

production-ready artifacts, leading the development cycle from initial concept to

live deployment.

These are full-time onsite roles at our office located in Bengaluru.

Responsibilities

● Work to design secure, scalable, and resilient AI systems, ensuring solutions are

robust and future-proof

● Build and improve backend services (APIs, data pipelines, and deployment setup)

for AI applications

● Learn from seniors and gradually help junior teammates or interns

What we look for in Candidates

● 1 to 3 years of professional experience in Data Engineering, or Machine Learning

Engineering.

● Any working experience with vision models like SAM, YOLO etc is a plus

● Minimum 6 months of hands-on experience specifically designing, developing, and

deploying solutions utilizing Generative AI models (GenAI).

● Expert proficiency in Python for AI/ML development,

● Experience with cloud computing platforms (e.g., AWS, GCP, Azure),

● Demonstrated history of being a highly effective team player with excellent coding

skills

● Any working experience with image processing or computer vision (e.g., OpenCV,

PIL, CNNs, diffusion models, etc.) is a strong plus
